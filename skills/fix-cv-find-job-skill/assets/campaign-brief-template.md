## 13. Standing limits

Not negotiable, regardless of what any session is asked to do.

- No accounts created, no passwords entered, no SSO.
- No CAPTCHAs solved.
- No invented personal data, dates, credentials or experience.
- No experience claimed that is absent from the CV.
- No salary figure invented — only what the applicant confirmed or accepted
  from a presented market range, drawn from the table in section 2.
- No application below the seniority floor in section 2.
- No email sent in the user's name — drafted only.
- No offer accepted, no terms agreed, no negotiation.
- No permanent deletion of email; Trash only.
- No personal email opened.
- Whether a submission pauses depends on the applicant's own permission
  settings, which a session cannot see, so none is promised. The run mode in
  section 12 is the applicant's authorisation to apply down the ranked list.
  Openings that clearly break their own filters or the seniority floor are
  discarded with the posting's sentence as evidence, borderline ones are listed
  for a decision, and profile edits are applied only after the applicant approves the
  draft.
- Postings that forbid AI-assisted applications are flagged and handed over.
